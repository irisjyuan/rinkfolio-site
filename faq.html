What is Rinkfolio?

Rinkfolio is a figure skating practice log. You keep track of the skills you're working on, count your attempts at the rink, and see how each skill is going over time. It also has a map of nearly 24,000 ice rinks in more than 100 countries, and you can follow other skaters.

Is it free?

Yes. Rinkfolio is free, with no ads and nothing to buy in the app.

Is it on iPhone or Android?

Both. On iPhone, get it from the App Store in the US (iOS 17 or later). On Android, it’s in beta on Google Play (Android 10 or later): to join, DM @rinkfolio on Instagram or email rinkfolioapp@gmail.com.

How many rinks does it have, and where?

Nearly 24,000 ice rinks in more than 100 countries: indoor rinks, outdoor rinks and wild ice spots like frozen ponds and lakes. The most are in Canada, Russia, the United States, Finland and Sweden. You can save rinks you want to visit, mark the ones you've skated at, set your home rinks, and share notes and photos about a rink.

Can I track jumps and spins and count attempts?

Yes. Add the skills you're working on, or pick them from ready-made groups: jumps from singles to quads, combinations, spins, turns, footwork, and Learn to Skate USA and ISI levels. At the rink, start a session and tap ✓ or ✗ after each try. It works with no signal. Each skill's page shows your usual, your best run, your attempts over recent sessions and how it has felt, from shaky to solid.

Is it for beginners, adults and coaches?

Rinkfolio is for figure skaters at any level and any age from 13 (14 in Quebec): beginners can start from the Learn to Skate USA and ISI levels, and adult skaters use it the same way. Coaches can use it for their own skating and keep notes, but there's no coach account or way to follow a student's practice yet.

Is my data private?

Your skills, practice counts and the details of every session are only for you, and your phone number is never shown to other skaters. Rinkfolio has no ads or ad tracking and doesn't sell your information. The privacy policy explains what's collected, who can see what, and your choices.

How do I suggest a rink that's missing?

In the app, go to Profile › Settings › Suggest a rink. Or search for it on the map, and when nothing matches, tap Can't find it? Suggest a rink. You can also email rinkfolioapp@gmail.com with the rink's name and town.

Something else? Email rinkfolioapp@gmail.com or see Support.
