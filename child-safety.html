Child safety standards

Rinkfolio, by Iris Yuan

Rinkfolio is for skaters 13 and older, and many of them are teenagers. We don't allow child sexual abuse or exploitation of any kind, by anyone, anywhere in the app.

What isn't allowed

  • Child sexual abuse material, or any photo, note or profile that sexualizes a minor.
  • Contacting a child for sexual purposes, grooming, or trying to meet a child in secret.
  • Threatening or pressuring a child for images, money or anything else (sextortion).
  • Anything else that exploits or endangers a child.

These are part of our terms of use, and breaking them gets the content removed and the account suspended.

How under-18s are kept safe

  • There are no direct messages, so no one can message a skater privately.
  • Under-18s' sessions and home rinks start shared only with friends (skaters they follow who follow them back), and their profile link shows only their @username.
  • Under-18s are never suggested to skaters they don't know.
  • Anyone can block a skater, and report a skater, note, photo or win.

How to report it

In the app: tap ••• on the skater's profile, or on the note, photo or win, then Report.

Without an account: email rinkfolioapp@gmail.com with "Child safety" in the subject, the skater's @username, and what you saw (the rink and the day help).

If a child is in immediate danger, call 911 or your local emergency number first. You can also report child sexual exploitation directly to NCMEC's CyberTipline.

What we do

Reports about a child at risk are looked at first, ahead of every other report, by a person. We hide and remove the content, suspend the account, and report child sexual abuse material to NCMEC's CyberTipline, as US law requires, and to the police where a child may be in danger. We keep what the law requires us to keep for investigators, and nothing more.

If someone shared an intimate image of you or another person under 18, see asking us to remove an intimate image. NCMEC's Take It Down can also help get it removed from other sites.

Contact for child safety

Rinkfolio's founder is responsible for child safety and handles these reports: rinkfolioapp@gmail.com.
