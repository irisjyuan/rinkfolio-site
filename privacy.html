Rinkfolio

Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

Rinkfolio is an app for finding ice rinks and keeping track of where you've skated. It's made by Iris Yuan ("we", "us"), based in California, USA. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. Questions: rinkfolioapp@gmail.com.

What we collect

Only what's needed to run the app. Apart from your phone number, the days you open the app, and crash reports, it's only what you add or allow as you use it:

  • Phone number. Used only to sign you in with a texted code. It is never shown to other skaters.
  • Your age, checked once. When you create an account, we check that you're old enough to use Rinkfolio (see Children): on iPhones that can, by asking Apple whether you're at least the minimum age (if you agree to share it; Apple says only yes or no, never your birthday), and otherwise by asking your birthday. We don't store your birthday or age: we keep only a record that your account passed the check, how, and when.
  • Profile. Your username, and anything you choose to add: name, profile photo, bio, disciplines, level, favorite jump and spin, signature move, skating club, when you started skating, and home rinks.
  • Who invited you. If you say a skater invited you when you sign up, we keep who it was. Only the two of you see it: you follow them, and they're told you joined from their invite.
  • Your lists and visits. Rinks you want to visit, rinks you've visited, and visits you log, with their dates, notes and photos.
  • Practice. The skills you keep track of, with their descriptions, categories, key things to remember, what counts as a good attempt for each (your standards) and which you've marked mastered, your practice plans, and which skills you practiced at each visit, with your notes.
  • Connections. Who you follow and who follows you (new accounts start out following @irisskating, Rinkfolio's founder, and are followed back; you can unfollow anytime), who you've blocked, reactions you leave on notes, photos and wins, and reports you send.
  • Location. If you allow it, your phone's location is used on your device to show nearby rinks and distances. To sort Explore by what's near you, a rough location (to about a kilometer) is sent with that request and used only to answer it. We don't store your location on our servers.
  • Notifications. If you turn on notifications, your phone's notification address (a token from Apple that only works for Rinkfolio on that phone) is saved to your account, so we can tell you about new followers and reactions. It's removed when you sign out on that phone. Your notification settings are saved to your account too. Reminders to log your next session, or that a session is still running, are set on your phone and aren't sent from our servers.
  • Days you open the app. Once a day, the app notes that your account opened it that day: just the date, not the time, where you were, or what you did. We use these only to see how many people keep coming back. They're kept for 13 months, and deleted with your account. You choose whether this happens (see Your choices).
  • Crash reports. If the app crashes or freezes, a report is sent so we can fix it: what the app was doing, the app and iOS versions, and the phone model. It doesn't include your name, username, phone number, account, IP address, location, or anything you've posted. You choose whether these are sent, too.
  • Reports and decisions. Reports you send (the reason; for illegal content, what you wrote about why; and for an intimate image shared without consent, the name you signed with and whether you asked for someone else), what we decided about them, and the notices we send you in the app about decisions: on your reports, or the reasons when we remove something of yours. If we suspend an account, we keep why.

We don't use advertising, ad tracking, or third-party analytics, and we don't sell your information. Crash reports are used only to fix problems, never to track you.

Who can see what

  • Other Rinkfolio users can see your username, profile, home rinks, want-to-visit list, who you follow and who follows you, your activity (rinks you save and home rinks you add or remove, unless you hide a row), your notes and photos about rinks (these are always public, like reviews), the rinks you've been to from sessions you share with Everyone, and any win you choose to share with Everyone (the skill's name and the win's words, and the rink and day only if that session is shared with Everyone too). Shared wins also appear in Explore, which shows other skaters' public notes and wins roughly in order of how near they are to you. Reactions show as counts; the skater whose note, photo or win you react to sees that it was you.
  • Your friends (skaters you follow who follow you back) can also see the rinks you've been to from sessions you share with Friends, and wins you share with Friends.
  • Only you can see sessions you mark Only me, the details of every session (its kind, length, name, skills, counts, notes, and any win you haven't shared), your skills, categories, practice plans and calendar, your phone number, who you've blocked, reports you've sent, and the notices we send you. When something you posted is removed after a report, you're never told who reported it.
  • Skaters you block, and skaters who block you, can't see each other's activity.

How we use it, and why we're allowed to

Only to run and improve the app. Privacy law asks us to say what each use rests on (its "legal basis"):

  • Running the app you signed up for (our agreement with you, the Terms): signing you in, your lists, visits, practice and profile, showing what you share, finding and following skaters, and the notifications you've turned on.
  • Keeping Rinkfolio safe (our legitimate interest in a safe community, and the law where it requires it): the word filter, daily limits, reviewing reports, removing content, suspending accounts, and telling you and reporters what we decided.
  • Crash reports and counting the days you open the app (your consent if your phone number is from the EU or EEA; otherwise our legitimate interest in fixing problems and seeing how many people keep using the app, and you can say no in Settings › Privacy).
  • Checking your age once (the law, and keeping children who are too young off the app).

We may email or text you only about your account or a report. We don't make decisions about you by automated means alone: every report is reviewed by a person.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores your account and app data and photos.
  • Twilio sends sign-in codes to your phone number.
  • Sentry receives crash reports.
  • Resend emails us when something is reported, with the usernames involved, the reason, and the reported note or photo, and once a day with the usernames of new skaters.
  • Apple provides maps and place names in the app, and delivers notifications to your phone (it receives the notification's text and your phone's notification token).

These services process data on our behalf and only to provide their service. We may also share information if required by law, or to protect the safety of our users.

Where your data is. Rinkfolio is run from the United States, and your data is stored and processed there by the services above, wherever you are.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you have an account. When you delete your account, your profile, lists, visits, notes, photos, practice, reactions, follows, blocks, notification settings and tokens, privacy choices, and the days you opened the app are deleted right away. Reports you sent about others may be kept, without your name attached, so we can deal with repeat problems. Turning off counting deletes the days already counted. When a photo is removed as an intimate image shared without consent, we keep its fingerprint (a code worked out from the file, which can't be turned back into the photo) so the same file can't be posted again. Backups held by our providers expire within a few weeks.

Suspended accounts. If we suspend an account for breaking the Terms, we remove what was reported (and, after reports, its notes, photos, sessions and profile), but we keep the account, its phone number and username, so the same number can't simply sign up again. Six months later (the time to disagree with the decision), the rest of its data is deleted; the account, its phone number, username, the reasons and the reports stay.

Your choices

  • Crash reports and counting the days you open the app: turn each on or off in Profile › Settings › Privacy. If your phone number is from the EU or EEA, both are off until you say yes (we ask once); otherwise both start on.
  • Download my data in Profile › Settings › Privacy: a copy of everything in your account, with your photos, in a zip file.
  • Edit or remove profile details, visits, notes and photos at any time in the app.
  • Choose who sees that you've been to a rink for each session: Everyone, Friends or Only me.
  • Hide a save or home rink change from your profile.
  • Turn location off in your phone's Settings.
  • Choose which notifications you get in Profile › Settings › Notifications, or turn them all off in your phone's Settings.
  • Delete your account in Profile › Settings › Delete account (email us if you need help signing in).
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, or ask us to correct it, by email.

If you're in the EU or EEA

You have these rights over your personal data, and we answer requests within a month:

  • Access and portability: a copy of your data in a format you can take elsewhere (Download my data, or ask us).
  • Correction of anything wrong (most of it you can edit in the app).
  • Deletion (Delete account in the app, or ask us).
  • Restriction: asking us to stop using some of your data while a question about it is settled.
  • Objection to uses that rest on our legitimate interest.
  • Withdrawing consent at any time, in Settings › Privacy, without affecting what came before.
  • Complaining to the data protection authority in your country (the list of them), though we'd like the chance to help first.

To use any of them, email rinkfolioapp@gmail.com. We may ask you to confirm it's your account.

If you're in Canada

Who's responsible. Rinkfolio's founder, Iris Yuan, is responsible for your personal information. Write to rinkfolioapp@gmail.com about anything in this policy.

Your information leaves Canada. It's stored and handled in the United States by the services listed above, which may only use it to provide their service to us.

Your rights. You can:

  • get a copy of your information: Profile › Settings › Privacy › Download my data, or ask us and we'll answer within 30 days;
  • correct it in Edit profile, or ask us;
  • take back a yes you gave, like crash reports, in Profile › Settings › Privacy;
  • delete your account in Profile › Settings.

If something goes wrong. If your information is ever exposed in a way that could seriously harm you, we'll tell you and the Privacy Commissioner of Canada as soon as we can.

Complaints. Write to us first; we'll answer within 30 days. You can also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

California residents

You have the right to know what personal information we collect, to delete it, to correct it, and not to be treated differently for using these rights. We don't sell or share personal information for advertising. Email us to make a request.

If you're under 18, you can remove anything you've posted: delete a note, photo or session in the app (its ••• or Edit), or email us and we'll remove it. Removing it takes it off Rinkfolio, but it can't undo copies someone else already made, such as a screenshot.

Do Not Track. Rinkfolio doesn't track you across other companies' apps or websites, and doesn't let anyone else do so through it, so it works the same whatever your browser's or phone's Do Not Track setting.

Children

Rinkfolio is for people 13 and older. In the EU and EEA, it's for people old enough to agree to an online service by themselves where they live (the age is set by each country):

  • 13: Belgium, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, Iceland, Latvia, Malta, Norway, Portugal, Sweden
  • 14: Austria, Bulgaria, Cyprus, Italy, Lithuania, Spain
  • 15: Czechia, France, Greece, Slovenia
  • 16: Croatia, Germany, Hungary, Ireland, Liechtenstein, Luxembourg, the Netherlands, Poland, Romania, Slovakia

In the United States, it's for people 18 and older in Mississippi and Tennessee, and 16 and older in Georgia and Ohio, where the law asks for a parent's consent below those ages.

We go by the country of your phone number, and in the US by its area code. New accounts are asked Apple whether they're old enough, or their birthday, and anyone younger can't create one; the account started by the texted code is deleted straight away. We don't knowingly collect information from children younger than this. If you believe a child that young is using Rinkfolio, email us and we'll look into it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and access to it is restricted so each person can only change their own information. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect yours.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For significant changes, we'll let you know in the app the next time you open it.
