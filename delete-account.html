Delete your Rinkfolio account

Rinkfolio, by Iris Yuan

In the app

Go to Profile › Settings › Delete account. Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app, or can't get into it, email rinkfolioapp@gmail.com with "Delete my account" in the subject and:

  • your @username;
  • the phone number you sign in with.

We'll check that the account is yours before deleting it, which may mean asking you to confirm from that phone number. Then we delete it and email you when it's done, within 30 days.

What's deleted

Your profile, lists, visits, sessions, notes, photos, practice, reactions, follows, blocks, notification settings, privacy choices, and the days you opened the app are deleted right away.

What's kept

  • Reports you sent about other skaters may be kept, without your name attached, so we can deal with repeat problems.
  • If a photo was removed as an intimate image shared without consent, we keep its fingerprint (a code worked out from the file, which can't be turned back into the photo) so the same file can't be posted again.
  • If an account was suspended for breaking the terms, its phone number, username, the reasons and the reports are kept, so the same number can't simply sign up again.
  • Backups held by our providers expire within a few weeks.

To keep a copy of your account first, use Download my data in Profile › Settings › Privacy before you delete it. The Privacy Policy has the details.
